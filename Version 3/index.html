01 — About Us

Your Aluminium & Glass Partner in RAK

Al Heera Aluminium & Glass supplies and installs high-quality aluminium and glass systems for residential, commercial, and industrial projects.

Based in Ras Al Khaimah, we blend superior craftsmanship with innovative design to deliver high-quality, durable, and aesthetically pleasing solutions. We are committed to enhancing architectural beauty across the region.

From the first consultation to the final installation, our technical department and engineering team manage the whole job. You deal with one dedicated company, from start to finish.

Commercial

Industrial
