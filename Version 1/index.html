01 — About Us

Your Aluminium & Glass Partner in RAK

Al Heera Aluminium & Glass supplies and installs high-quality aluminium and glass systems for residential, commercial, and industrial projects.

Based in Ras Al Khaimah, we combine skilled craftsmanship with modern design to deliver work that is durable, well finished, and suited to the local climate.

From the first consultation to the final installation, our team manages the whole job. You deal with one company, from start to finish.

Who We Work With

Residential

Villas, apartments, and home renovations — windows, doors, partitions, and balcony railings.

Commercial

Offices, shops, and showrooms — glass fronts, facades, and interior glass walls.

Industrial

Warehouses and facilities — durable aluminium systems built for heavy daily use.
